# Design System: Anthropic Editorial Research Aesthetic

## Vision & Philosophy

The FRA Intelligence Workstation visual design system is built in the spirit of **Anthropic's calm, literary, and human-centered design language**. It transforms a technical transformer diagnostic tool from a harsh, dark cockpit into a **dignified research laboratory environment** reminiscent of seminal scientific journals, thoughtful monographs, and Claude's quiet editorial poise.

### Foundational Principles

1. **Warmth & Scholarly Poise**: The canvas uses a warm, unbleached linen ground (`#FAF8F5`) and warm white card modules (`#FFFFFF`), replacing cold neon glow with tactile warmth, generous breathability, and dignified clarity.
2. **Literary Typography**: Headings and titles use classic editorial serif typography (`Newsreader` and `Lora`) paired with `Source Sans 3` for interface copy, communicating deep intellectual credibility and scientific authority.
3. **Restrained Terracotta Accent**: The primary signature accent is Claude's unmistakable warm terracotta/clay (`#CC785C`), complemented by muted forest sage for healthy baseline states and warm ochre for advisory warnings.
4. **Structured Information Architecture**:
   - **Landing First**: A warm, welcoming front door (`/`) introducing the apparatus physics, standards grounding (IEEE C57.149 & IEC 60076-18), live fleet telemetry snapshot, and clear pathways into all workspaces.
   - **Operate Smoothly**: The Workbench (`/analysis`), Fleet Records (`/history`), and Standards Reference (`/about`) are seamlessly connected via an unobtrusive top navigation bar.
5. **Precision Without Glare**: High-density numerical data (frequencies, decibel shifts, correlation coefficients) is rendered in clean tabular `JetBrains Mono`.
