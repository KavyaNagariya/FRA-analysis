03 // IEEE STD C57.149 & IEC 60076-18 PHYSICAL SUB-BAND PARTITIONING

VIEW COMPLETE MANUAL
SUB-BAND 1: LOW FREQUENCY < 2 kHz
DOMINANT CIRCUIT PARAMETERS

Governed by core magnetizing inductance ($L_m$) and bulk winding resistance ($R_s$). Resonances dictated by magnetic core geometry.

DETECTABLE DEFECTS
  • Core lamination short circuits
  • Residual magnetization / DC bias
  • Core grounding strap detachment
  • Main magnetic frame displacement
NORM THRESHOLD: R² ≥ 0.95 | ΔdB < 1.0
SUB-BAND 2: MID FREQUENCY 2 kHz – 100 kHz
DOMINANT CIRCUIT PARAMETERS

Governed by winding leakage inductance ($L_\sigma$) and inter-disk / series capacitances ($C_s, C_g$). Multiple sharp anti-resonance poles.

DETECTABLE DEFECTS
  • Radial winding buckling (hoop stress)
  • Axial winding displacement / collapse
  • Clamping pressure loss & disk tilt
  • Inter-turn insulation breakdown
NORM THRESHOLD: R² ≥ 0.95 | Δf < 2.0%
SUB-BAND 3: HIGH FREQUENCY > 100 kHz (to 2 MHz)
DOMINANT CIRCUIT PARAMETERS

Governed by internal tap leads, bushing capacitance ($C_b$), external grounding braid inductance, and terminal connections.

DETECTABLE DEFECTS
  • De-energized Tap Changer (DETC) misalignment
  • Bushing turret lead shift
  • Field test grounding braid loops
  • External terminal lead damage
NORM THRESHOLD: R² ≥ 0.90 | ΔdB < 2.0
