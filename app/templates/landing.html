Rigorous foundations in apparatus physics.

Rather than treating diagnostics as a black-box machine learning classifier, FRA Intelligence grounds every finding in observable circuit parameters, resonance shifts, and international standards.

Distributed RLC Ladder

Every transformer winding acts as a distributed passive ladder of series inductance ($L_s$), mutual coupling ($M$), and disk capacitances ($C_s, C_g$). Mechanical shifts change geometry, which directly alters resonant frequencies.

Read circuit derivation

IEEE Sub-Band Standards

Strict adherence to IEEE Std C57.149-2012 and IEC 60076-18 sub-band criteria isolates core displacement (< 2 kHz) from winding axial/radial distortion (2 kHz – 100 kHz) and lead looseness (> 100 kHz).
