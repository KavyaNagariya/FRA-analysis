STANDARDS & DIAGNOSTIC CRITERIA

Normative Sub-Band Frequency Boundaries and Physics Interpretation Guides

CORE SUB-BAND (< 2 kHz) IEEE C57.149

Dominated by magnetizing inductance ($L_m$) and core grounding. Deviations indicate core magnetic circuit degradation, short-circuited laminations, or residual magnetization.

WINDING INTERACTION (2k - 100 kHz) IEEE C57.149

Determined by winding series capacitances and mutual inductances. Resonance shifts in this band signify mechanical deformation: axial displacement or radial hoop buckling.

HIGH FREQUENCY (> 100 kHz) IEEE C57.149

Dominated by tap changer leads, bushing capacitance, and grounding leads. Deviations correspond to lead shifts, grounding resistance fluctuations, or bushing issues.
